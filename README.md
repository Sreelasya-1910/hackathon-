# Overview

The application allows all users to view the current status of the arenas, including whether a court is **occupied, vacant, or booked for official or coaching purposes**.

Staff members and Sports In-Charges can reserve courts for official purposes using their **staff ID**. In accordance with the existing rules, individuals using a court before an official booking will be required to vacate the court once the booking becomes effective, regardless of whether they are using the application.

A court reserved for official or coaching purposes will remain unavailable until the concerned staff member updates its status to **Free**.

Users may join a queue for an occupied or reserved court and **claim the court** once the current occupants have vacated it. After leaving the court, users may mark its status as **Empty**, allowing the next person in the queue to use it.

If a court is **empty and there is no one in the queue**, users can directly walk in and claim the court.

To prevent spam, false claims, and misuse of the system, the **Claim** and **Flag as Empty** features will be accessible only when the user is physically present within the **SNCC premises**.
